## What are cookies?

Cookies are text files stored on your device by a website's server. Each cookie contains a unique identifier, website's domain name, and other data to associate you. Cookies and other related measures are commonly used to track internet users across websites and devices, usually for marketing purposes.

## Cookies by fortrabbit

The cookies fortrabbit sets itself on its web properties (www, docs, blog and dashboard) are functional cookies. They help to offer a convenient experience and let us recognize that you are logged in, or have set a certain color scheme. These cookies are created and used only by us and not shared with any third party.

## Advertising cookies

A Google Ads remarketing tag runs on [www.fortrabbit.com](https://www.fortrabbit.com), [docs.fortrabbit.com](https://docs.fortrabbit.com) and [blog.fortrabbit.com](https://blog.fortrabbit.com). The dashboard carries no advertising tag. It sets advertising cookies, both on the `fortrabbit.com` domain and on Google's own domains, so that fortrabbit ads can be shown to the same browser on other websites.

The tag is not loaded for visitors in jurisdictions that require opt-in consent for advertising cookies — the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Brazil and Quebec. It is also not loaded when the location is unknown, or when the browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. The location is determined from the IP address via MaxMind.

Where the tag loads, Google can set these cookies:

| Cookie | Domain | Duration | Purpose |
|--------|--------|----------|---------|
| `_gcl_au` | `fortrabbit.com` | 90 days | Links an ad click to a later visit |
| `_gcl_aw` | `fortrabbit.com` | 90 days | Stores the click ID of an ad click |
| `IDE` | `doubleclick.net` | up to 13 months | Shows ads to the same browser on other websites |
| `test_cookie` | `doubleclick.net` | 15 minutes | Checks whether the browser accepts cookies |

The durations are Google's defaults. Opt-out routes are listed on the [privacy page](/benefits/privacy).

Ad click parameters in the URL, such as `gclid` and `utm_source`, are passed from www to the signup page in the link itself. fortrabbit stores them in no cookie.

## Cookies by fortrabbit sub-contractors

Certain functionalities of our services are not provided by us directly, but integrated via a third party. This includes but is not limited to:

- Git provider
- Payment processing
- Support chat
- Status page

These services are storing their own cookies and have their own respective policies. We aim to make the usage of these third-party integrations optional where possible and make it visible wherever a third-party service is used.

## How to delete cookies?

You can restrict or block cookies through your browser settings.
